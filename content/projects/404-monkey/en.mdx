## The problem

Invoicing properly takes different rules depending on whether you are a freelancer, a company or a non-profit. The product offers one profile per status.

## What I built

A Next.js 16 application with React 19 and Tailwind 4. Supabase handles authentication, Postgres and row-level security (RLS), Stripe handles payments. Prices follow tiers based on the number of clients, and the voluntary contribution stays strictly opt-in.

## The problem

Stopping a machine too late is expensive, and so is stopping it too early. The project tries to predict a failure 24 hours ahead from 15 measured variables.

## What I built

Four models compared: logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost and a multilayer perceptron. Interpretability goes through SHAP. The best model is served by a FastAPI service and a Streamlit dashboard.

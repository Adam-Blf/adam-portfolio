## The problem

In a mental health institution in the Paris region, the regulatory PMSI files come in heterogeneous formats, and a format error costs time to fix.

## What I built

A desktop application in Python: a tested business core, a pywebview interface, a FastAPI backend, an XGBoost module, identifier consistency checks and normalised exports. The starting requirement was simple: everything stays local, no external API. The formats cover the PSY, MCO, SSR and HAD fields.

## The problem

A compliance copilot that invents a legal article is worse than no copilot. Vigie answers on DORA, the AI Act, GDPR and AML-CFT by citing the exact article, and says plainly when the texts do not answer.

## What I built

A hybrid search on Qdrant, a FastAPI service, a model exported to ONNX and quantised to int8, and a safeguard that blocks prompt injections. A version only goes to production if it passes the evaluation: runs are tracked in MLflow, load is replayed with Locust, deployment targets a k3s cluster described with Terraform and Docker Compose, and the pipeline runs in GitHub Actions.

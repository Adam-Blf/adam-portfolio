## The problem

Publishing health data, even aggregated, means knowing whether a person or an institution can be found in it.

## What I built

A Python pipeline that ingests the FINESS register, checks data quality, then anonymises it. An engine measures re-identification risk with k-anonymity and l-diversity, and applies generalisation and suppression until the level is acceptable. Tests run with pytest.

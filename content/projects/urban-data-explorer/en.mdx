## The problem

Urban data for Paris is scattered, in different formats and of uneven quality. The project gathers it, cleans it and makes it readable on a map. It was the exam project of the Data Architecture course in the Master's.

## What I built

A pipeline in Bronze, Silver and Gold layers. Ingestion and transformation with Python and Polars, geometries with Shapely, partitioned Parquet storage, queries with DuckDB then PostgreSQL, Kafka streams, a FastAPI service protected by JWT, a React map with MapLibre. Four families of sources, 83 in total. CI runs ruff and pytest.

## The problem

Building, on a realistic e-commerce dataset, a chain that starts from raw data and ends in aggregates ready for analysis.

## What I built

A medallion architecture: raw data arrives as a Kafka stream, PySpark cleans and structures it, Hive holds the Gold layer aggregates. The stack also includes Hadoop, FastAPI and Streamlit.

## The problem

Showing that market data can flow from the raw stream to the screen, with indicators computed on the fly.

## What I built

WebSocket ingestion from two exchanges, Kafka in KRaft mode 3.7, computation of the VWAP, the 20-period moving average (SMA-20) and the volume z-score in Node.js and TypeScript, a REST and Socket.IO API, a React 18 dashboard. Everything starts with Docker Compose, in four services.
